This image is a **Sales Dashboard**. It contains KPI cards, filters, and **three main charts**: a bar chart, a pie chart, and a line chart.

### 1. Bar Chart – Sum by Sub-Category

**What it shows:**

* The **X-axis** contains product sub-categories such as Accessories, Appliances, Binders, Chairs, Phones, Storage, etc.
* The **Y-axis** represents the total value.
* Each bar represents one sub-category.
* A taller bar means a higher total value.

**Purpose:**
It helps compare the performance/value of different product sub-categories.

This is the **pie chart in the center**.

It divides profit among three main categories:

* **Furniture**

* **Technology**

The pie has three slices, representing each category's share of the total profit.

**What it shows:**

* A larger slice = larger contribution to the total profit.

**Purpose:**
The pie chart is useful for understanding **how the total profit is distributed among product categories**.
